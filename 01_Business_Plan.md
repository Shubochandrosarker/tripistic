# Tripistic Business Plan

## 1. Business Summary

**Tripistic** is an AI-native tour operations platform built for independent guides, small tour companies, local activity providers, and experience operators.

Tripistic should not compete as just another booking engine. The market already has many booking platforms. The winning position is:

> AI growth co-pilot + all-in-one operations platform for solo guides and small tour operators.

## 2. Market Opportunity

The tour, activity, and attractions sector is large, growing, and still under-digitized.

Key opportunity signals:

- Many operators still run bookings manually.
- Small operators often use spreadsheets, WhatsApp, email, calendars, payment links, and disconnected tools.
- Existing platforms are often expensive, fee-heavy, or too complex.
- AI is changing how travelers discover destinations, experiences, and local activities.
- Operators need better direct-booking tools because OTA dependency is expensive.

## 3. Target Customers

### Primary Segment

Independent guides and small operators with:

- 1–10 team members
- Local tours, activities, classes, rentals, or experiences
- Manual booking process or weak booking system
- Heavy WhatsApp/email communication
- Limited technical skill
- Price sensitivity
- Need for growth, not only scheduling

### Secondary Segment

Growing operators with:

- Multiple guides
- Multi-day tours
- Seasonal demand
- OTA listings
- Need for scheduling, waivers, CRM, reporting, and direct-booking growth

## 4. Core Pain Points

| Pain | Business Impact | Tripistic Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Manual bookings | Lost sales, double bookings | Real-time booking widget |
| OTA dependency | 20–30% commission loss | Direct-booking engine |
| High software fees | Lower margin | Flat pricing, 0% direct commission |
| Disconnected tools | Admin waste | All-in-one dashboard |
| Manual guest messages | No-shows, support overload | Automated email/SMS/WhatsApp |
| Weak analytics | No growth clarity | AI Growth Dashboard |
| Seasonal demand | Empty slots or underpriced tours | Forecasting + pricing insights |
| Language barriers | Lost international guests | Multilingual AI booking agent |

## 5. Business Model

Tripistic should use a flat SaaS subscription model.

### Recommended Model

- 0% commission on direct bookings
- No customer-facing booking surcharge
- Monthly and annual plans
- Paid add-ons for AI usage, messaging, white-label, and advanced integrations
- Optional migration/setup service

## 6. Revenue Streams

1. **Monthly SaaS subscription**
2. **Annual plans** with discount
3. **AI usage upgrades** for high-volume operators
4. **Messaging credits** for SMS/WhatsApp
5. **White-label agency plan**
6. **Migration/setup service**
7. **Marketplace/add-ons** later
8. **Payment partner revenue share** later, only if transparent

## 7. Differentiation

Tripistic’s strongest differentiators:

- AI Growth Dashboard
- Demand forecasting
- 0% direct-booking commission
- Transparent pricing
- Multilingual AI booking agent
- AI itinerary builder
- No-show prediction
- Guide scheduling optimization
- Direct-booking growth system
- Agent-commerce readiness for ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini-style booking discovery

## 8. Strategic Position

Tripistic should attack three groups:

1. Operators with no booking system
2. Operators frustrated with FareHarbor/Peek/Rezdy/Checkfront pricing
3. New post-2022 operators who need a simple modern system

## 9. Business Goal

The short-term goal is not to beat enterprise platforms. The goal is to own the small-operator AI operations category.

**Best initial target:**

> 100 paying operators with less than 5% monthly churn before expanding into advanced AI and marketplace features.

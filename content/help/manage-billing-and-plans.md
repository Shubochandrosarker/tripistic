Billing is managed by the workspace **Owner**. Admins cannot access it.

## Change plan

**Settings → Billing → Change plan.**

| Change | When it applies | Charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upgrade | Immediately | Prorated for the remaining term |
| Downgrade | Next renewal | None now |
| Monthly → annual | Immediately | Annual total, less unused monthly credit |
| Annual → monthly | Next renewal | None now |

Annual billing saves 20%.

## Add or remove seats

Seats are named users with a login. Adding a seat is prorated and immediate. Removing a seat applies at the next renewal.

Guides who receive manifests without logging in do not consume a seat.

## Update payment details

**Settings → Billing → Payment method.** Card details are handled by Stripe; Tripistic never sees or stores the full number.

If a payment fails we retry and email the owner. Repeated failures restrict access before suspension — update the card promptly.

## Invoices

Every invoice is downloadable from **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Add your VAT or tax number and billing address there so they appear on the invoice; retroactive reissues are possible but slower than getting it right once.

## Cancel

**Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription.** Cancellation stops renewal at the end of the current term — you keep access until then.

After the term ends, your data remains exportable for **30 days**. [Export it](/help/export-your-data) before that window closes.

## Refunds

- Annual plans: full refund within 30 days of the charge.
- Monthly plans: full refund within 14 days of a first charge, if the workspace was not substantively used.
- Billing errors — duplicate charges, charges after cancellation, wrong tier — refunded in full, always.

Full detail in the [Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).

## Enterprise

Enterprise agreements are governed by the order form. Contact [sales@tripistic.com](mailto:sales@tripistic.com) for seat changes, invoicing terms, or a negotiated SLA.

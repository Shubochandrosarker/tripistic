# Tripistic Pricing and Packaging

## 1. Pricing Strategy

Tripistic should use pricing as a competitive weapon.

The main promise:

> 0% commission on direct bookings. No customer-facing booking surcharge.

This attacks the biggest weakness of many competitors: high percentage booking fees.

## 2. Recommended Plans

| Plan | Price | Target Customer | Best For |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Starter | $19/month | Solo guides | Simple direct bookings |
| Operator | $69/month | Small operators | Full operations |
| Growth | $99/month | Growing teams | AI + automation + integrations |
| Agency | $199+/month | Agencies/consultants | Multi-client management |

## 3. Starter Plan

### Price

$19/month or $190/year

### Target

Solo guide, local tour host, new operator.

### Features

- 1 user
- 3 tour products
- Booking widget
- Stripe payments
- Basic availability calendar
- Email confirmations
- Basic waiver
- Basic CRM
- Basic AI insights
- 0% direct booking commission

## 4. Operator Plan

### Price

$69/month or $690/year

### Target

Small tour company with multiple products or guides.

### Features

- Up to 5 users
- Unlimited tour products
- Advanced availability
- Deposits/installments
- Digital waivers
- SMS/WhatsApp reminders via credits
- Guide scheduling
- Guest CRM
- Review automation
- AI Growth Dashboard
- Basic OTA sync

## 10. Pricing Copy

Use this message:

> Keep more of every direct booking. Tripistic gives you bookings, payments, guest communication, waivers, and AI growth insights for one flat monthly price — with 0% commission on direct bookings.

## 11. Pricing Warning

Do not compete only by being cheap.

Tripistic must compete on:

- Lower direct-booking cost
- Better AI insights
- Simpler operation
- Faster setup
- No lock-in
- Better growth guidance

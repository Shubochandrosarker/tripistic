# Tripistic — Competitor Alternative Pages Plan

> Bottom-funnel pages that convert fee-frustrated switchers. Rules: every claim dated + sourced (vendors change pricing), no fabricated review quotes, always include "when [competitor] is the better fit" for credibility, always offer **free migration**. Legal hygiene: nominative use of competitor names only; no trademarked logos without review.

## Shared page template

1. **H1:** "[Competitor] Alternative for Small Tour Operators — 0% Direct-Booking Commission"
2. Fee math box (their model vs Tripistic flat plan at $50k/$100k/$250k direct revenue) → links to calculator
3. Side-by-side table (pricing model, consumer surcharge, AI insights, waivers, guide scheduling, lock-in, data ownership, migration)
4. "What you give up" honesty section (their real strengths)
5. Migration offer (tours, availability, Stripe, widget, templates, customer import — from `06_GTM_And_Sales_System.md`)
6. FAQ with schema (fees, switching time, data export)
7. CTA: Start free trial · Request free migration
8. Internal links: `/pricing`, commission calculator, 2–3 niche pages, comparison page

## Per-competitor pages

### `/alternatives/fareharbor` — kw: *fareharbor alternatives, fareharbor fees*
- **Attack:** 6–8% consumer-facing checkout fee (conversion killer — extra costs are the #1 cart-abandonment cause), 2% API fee backlash, website ownership up to $5k/yr, Booking.com data-sharing anxiety, multi-year lock-in.
- **Concede:** scale, 24/7 support, OTA integrations, polished checkout, new native Agent.
- **Angle:** "Keep your website, your customer data, and 100% of your direct revenue."

### `/alternatives/peek-pro` — kw: *peek pro alternatives, peek pro pricing*
- **Attack:** ~6% direct commission, opaque fee calculation, learning curve, mid-season support lag.
- **Concede:** best-in-class POS, genuinely strong AI pricing/Copilot.
- **Angle:** "AI growth insights without paying 6% for them." Position our AI as *prescriptive growth*, not just pricing.

### `/alternatives/rezdy` — kw: *rezdy alternatives*
- **Attack:** $49–249/mo **plus 3%**, no native AI, "core product hasn't changed in years", forced plan upgrades.
- **Concede:** strongest OTA channel manager/reseller network (be honest that our OTA sync is roadmap Phase 9).
- **Angle:** "Modern AI-native operations at a flat price — stop paying twice."

### `/alternatives/bokun` — kw: *bokun alternatives*
- **Attack:** Viator ownership = customer-data/lock-in concern, dated UI, limited reporting, no native AI.
- **Concede:** lowest fees among incumbents (1.5%), Viator ecosystem access.
- **Angle:** "Own your customer relationships. Grow direct, not just on Viator."

### `/alternatives/checkfront` — kw: *checkfront alternatives*
- **Attack:** $99/mo **+3%** double toll, publicly ships no native AI, slow innovation.
- **Concede:** solid bookings+rentals coverage.
- **Angle:** "Cleaner, smarter, AI-native — for less."

### `/alternatives/xola` — kw: *xola alternatives*
- **Attack:** transaction-fee model, recurring bug complaints, overkill for very small operators.
- **Concede:** conversion-optimized checkout, remarketing tools.
- **Angle:** "Built for solo guides and small teams — without per-booking tolls."

### EU extras (wave 2): `/alternatives/trekksoft` (real cost ~$150–175/mo + setup fee), `/alternatives/regiondo` (opaque/expensive, EU angle + GDPR + multilingual roadmap).

"Best FareHarbor/Peek/Rezdy alternatives for small tour operators (2026)" — ranks honest options including cheap ones (Bookeo) with fit notes; Tripistic positioned for the small-operator + AI-growth case. Hubs that admit alternatives earn AI-assistant citations.

## Measurement
Per page: organic entrances → calculator use → migration requests → trials → paid. A page that ranks but doesn't produce migration requests gets its fee-math box and CTA rewritten first.

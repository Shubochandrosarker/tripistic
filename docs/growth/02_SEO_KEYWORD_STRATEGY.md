## 3. AEO / AI-search strategy (33% of travelers discover activities via AI)

1. **Structured everything:** Product/FAQ/HowTo JSON-LD on every marketing page; clean headings; quotable single-sentence answers ("Tripistic charges 0% commission on direct bookings.").
2. **Comparison honesty:** AI assistants synthesize across sources — factual, sourced fee tables outrank hype and earn citations.
3. **Publish the data:** commission calculators with methodology, fee-comparison tables kept dated and accurate → the pages LLMs cite.
4. **llms.txt + docs openness** on the marketing site; fast, crawlable, no JS-walled content.
5. **Product side (later, Phase 9+):** operator tour pages with Schema.org `TouristTrip`/`Product`/`Offer` markup = "your tours, readable by ChatGPT/Gemini" — a product feature that doubles as an SEO/AEO story nobody else tells small operators.

## 4. Local SEO angle (content we teach = product we sell)

Operators' #1 free channel is Google Business Profile + local pack. Own the teaching content: `local seo for tour operators`, `google business profile for tours`, `how to rank for "food tour [city]"`. Every guide ends in the same funnel: audit tool → email → trial. This builds topical authority that supports the commercial pages.

## 5. Execution order (first 90 days of content)

1. **Weeks 1–3:** pricing page, 3 alternative pages (FareHarbor, Peek, Rezdy), OTA commission calculator + revenue-loss calculator.
2. **Weeks 4–6:** remaining alternative pages (Bókun, Checkfront, Xola), comparison pages, alternatives hub.
3. **Weeks 7–10:** 6–8 niche `/solutions/*` pages, GTTD readiness content + checker tool.
4. **Weeks 11–13:** AI cluster (`/features/ai-growth-dashboard` + 3 guides), GDPR page, review-growth guide + review-reply free tool.
5. Continuous: Capterra/G2/Trustpilot profiles + review seeding from beta users (decisive discovery channel per research).

**Measurement:** GSC queries → map to page map; leading KPIs = calculator completions, tool email captures, trial signups per page; do not chase rankings without conversions.

# Tripistic — Product Growth Roadmap

> Growth work sequenced against the build phases in `docs/08_PHASE_ROADMAP.md`. Product-led rule: every growth asset must either capture a lead, shorten time-to-first-booking, or make an operator's success visible.

## 1. Immediate growth features (now — parallel with Phases 1–2, no booking engine required)

| Item | Why now |
|---|---|
| Marketing site: home, `/pricing`, 3 alternative pages (FareHarbor, Peek, Rezdy) | Bottom-funnel demand exists today; pages compound |
| OTA Commission Calculator + Booking Fee Calculator (`/tools`) | Zero-cost lead engines; power the fee-math on every page |
| Waitlist + free-migration request form | Collect switchers before the booking engine ships |
| Capterra/G2/Trustpilot profiles created | Review platforms are the decisive discovery channel |
| Community presence (Tourpreneur, Arival, operator FB/Reddit groups) | Founder-led; source of 20–30 validation interviews (Phase 0 GTM tasks) |
| Public roadmap page + changelog | Converts "is this real?" skepticism; supports beta recruiting |
| Onboarding checklist in-product (Phase 1 — built) | Activation scaffolding from day one |

## 5. Final recommendation — fastest path to 100 paying operators

1. **Sell the switch, not the software.** The sharpest, cheapest demand is fee-frustrated FareHarbor/Peek/Rezdy users searching "[x] alternatives" plus the calculators that quantify their pain. Ship those 5 pages + 2 calculators **before** the booking engine is finished; collect migration requests into a waitlist.
2. **Founder-led onboarding for the first 20 beta operators** (from waitlist + communities) at beta pricing ($19/$49), free migration included, in exchange for a review (Capterra/G2) and a case-study number. Manual onboarding is the product research.
3. **Ship the backbone in strict order (Phases 1–6)** so beta operators take real money through Tripistic — retention comes from processed revenue, not features.
4. **Ship AI Growth Dashboard (Phase 7) only after ~20 operators have booking data**, then market exclusively around documented outcomes ("found X an extra $N/month"). One true number beats every AI adjective.
5. **Convert content flywheel:** every tool lead → segmented drip → migration offer; every happy beta → review + case study → alternative pages get proof → conversion rises. Target thresholds from `03_MVP_Roadmap.md`: 100 waitlist leads → 20 beta → 100 paying at <5% churn.

**What NOT to do:** paid ads before message-market fit; OTA integrations before direct booking works; leading with dynamic pricing; publishing niche pages for capabilities that don't exist yet; competing with Capterra for "tour operator software" head terms in year one.

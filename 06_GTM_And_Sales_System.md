# Tripistic GTM and Sales System

## 1. Go-To-Market Strategy

Tripistic should target small operators who are:

- Still offline/manual
- New and looking for their first system
- Frustrated with high booking fees
- Dependent on OTAs
- Using multiple disconnected tools
- Interested in AI but not technical

## 2. Core Offer

> Switch to Tripistic and manage bookings, payments, waivers, guest messages, and AI growth insights from one clean dashboard — with 0% direct-booking commission.

## 3. Main Customer Segments

### Segment 1: Offline Operators

Pain:

- Manual bookings
- Spreadsheets
- WhatsApp/email chaos
- No online payment flow

Offer:

- “Launch online booking in one day.”

### Segment 2: Fee-Frustrated Operators

Pain:

- High booking fees
- Customer surcharge complaints
- Expensive platform lock-in

Offer:

- “Move to flat pricing and keep more direct revenue.”

### Segment 3: Growth-Focused Operators

Pain:

- Don’t know what to improve
- Weak analytics
- Heavy OTA dependency

Offer:

- “Use AI to find your next booking opportunity.”

### Segment 4: Agencies/Consultants

Pain:

- Need tools for tourism clients
- Want recurring revenue
- Need branded reporting

Offer:

- “White-label AI booking and growth system for travel clients.”

### Review Platforms

Build presence on:

- Capterra

### Direct Outreach

Targets:

- Small tour websites with weak/no booking widget
- Operators using inquiry forms only
- Operators ranking locally but no direct booking
- New tour companies

## 5. Sales Funnel

### Landing Page Flow

1. Pain headline
2. 0% direct-commission promise
3. AI Growth Dashboard demo
4. Booking/payment/waiver modules
5. Competitor fee comparison
6. Migration offer
7. Pricing
8. Free trial / demo CTA

### Lead Magnet Ideas

- “Tour Operator Direct Booking Checklist”
- “OTA Commission Calculator”
- “AI Growth Audit for Tour Operators”
- “Booking Fee Savings Calculator”

## 6. Migration Offer

Very important for switchers.

Offer:

> Free guided migration from FareHarbor, Rezdy, Peek, Bókun, or Checkfront for qualified operators.

Migration includes:

- Tour/product setup
- Availability setup
- Stripe connection
- Booking widget installation
- Basic email templates
- Customer import
- Waiver setup

## 7. Sales Messaging

### Main Hook

> Stop paying commission on your own direct bookings.

### AI Hook

> Know exactly which tours to promote, when to add slots, and where your bookings are coming from.

### Operations Hook

> Replace spreadsheets, payment links, waivers, calendars, and manual reminders with one clean system.

## 8. Founder-Led Sales Script

Use this structure:

1. “How are you handling bookings today?”
2. “How much comes from direct vs OTA?”
3. “Are customers paying booking fees?”
4. “How much time do you spend on reminders and admin?”
5. “Do you know which tours are most profitable?”
6. Show Tripistic workflow.
7. Offer setup/migration.

## 9. Early KPI Targets

| KPI | Target |
|---|---:|
| Waitlist leads | 100 |
| Beta operators | 20 |
| Paying operators | 100 |
| Monthly churn | Under 5% |
| Direct booking increase | 20%+ for active users |
| Admin time saved | 5+ hours/month minimum |

## 10. GTM Warning

Do not market Tripistic as “AI booking software” only.

Better:

> AI-powered direct-booking and operations platform for small tour operators.

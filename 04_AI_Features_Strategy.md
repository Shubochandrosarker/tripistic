# Tripistic AI Features Strategy

## 1. AI Strategy Summary

Tripistic’s AI should not be decorative. It must directly help operators:

- Get more bookings
- Reduce admin work
- Reduce no-shows
- Price smarter
- Understand demand
- Serve guests faster
- Grow direct revenue

The best AI positioning:

> Tripistic is the AI growth brain for tour operators.

## 2. Flagship AI Feature: AI Growth Dashboard

### Purpose

The AI Growth Dashboard tells operators what is happening and what to do next.

### Inputs

- Booking data
- Revenue data
- Occupancy
- Tour performance
- Channel source
- Customer data
- Review data
- Cancellation/no-show data
- Weather/event data later

### Outputs

- Sales growth actions
- Demand forecast
- Underperforming tour alerts
- Pricing suggestions
- Channel performance insights
- Direct-booking improvement suggestions
- Repeat customer opportunities
- Review/reputation recommendations

### Example Insight Cards

- “Your sunset tour sells out faster than other products. Add one more Friday slot.”
- “Your weekday occupancy is below 35%. Offer a weekday private-group package.”
- “Viator brings volume, but direct bookings produce 22% higher net margin.”
- “Guests who book 5+ days ahead cancel less. Promote early-bird booking.”

## 3. AI Booking Agent

### Purpose

A multilingual AI assistant that answers guest questions and helps complete bookings.

### Required Capabilities

- Read live availability
- Recommend tours
- Answer pricing questions
- Explain cancellation policy
- Send payment links
- Send waiver links
- Handoff to human
- Support email, chat, SMS, WhatsApp later

### Safety Rule

The AI must never invent availability, pricing, legal claims, pickup information, or refund promises.

It must always use verified system data.

## 4. AI Itinerary Builder

### Purpose

Help operators turn guest inquiries into bookable packages.

### Use Cases

- Multi-day tours
- Private tours
- Family trips
- Corporate group trips
- Destination packages
- Add-on suggestions

### Output

- Day-by-day itinerary
- Pricing estimate
- Tour/product bundle
- Pickup notes
- Guest preparation notes
- Payment link

## 5. Demand Forecasting

### Purpose

Predict demand so operators can plan pricing, staffing, and availability.

### Forecast Inputs

- Historical bookings
- Seasonality
- Day of week
- Lead time
- Weather later
- Local events later
- OTA trends later

### Forecast Outputs

- High-demand dates
- Low-demand dates
- Suggested extra slots
- Suggested promotions
- Staffing recommendations

## 6. No-Show Prediction

### Purpose

Reduce no-shows and last-minute cancellations.

### Signals

- Unpaid balance
- No waiver signed
- No reminder opened/clicked
- Late booking
- Past customer history
- Group size
- Weather risk

### Actions

- Send stronger reminder
- Request deposit/full payment
- Ask for confirmation
- Alert operator

## 7. AI Dynamic Pricing

### Recommended Approach

Do not start with automatic surge pricing.

Start with:

- AI pricing suggestions
- Operator approval
- Price floor/cap
- Clear explanation
- Manual override

### Why

Dynamic pricing can create customer backlash if it feels unfair. Tripistic should position this as transparent revenue optimization, not hidden surge pricing.

## 9. Agent-Commerce Readiness

Future opportunity:

Make operator inventory discoverable and bookable by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and future travel agents.

This needs:

- Clean product APIs
- Structured availability
- Pricing endpoints
- Booking confirmation endpoint
- Cancellation/refund policy data
- Schema/JSON-LD
- MCP/API bridge later
